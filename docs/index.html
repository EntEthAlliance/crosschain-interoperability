Crosschain Interoperability

Our focus

Standardising DLT interoperability

The EEA Crosschain Interoperability Working Group is dedicated to standardising interoperability across blockchain and distributed ledger technologies. Our specifications support the public Ethereum Mainnet and EVM-compatible networks — including consortium blockchains, optimistic rollups and zk rollups — as well as non-EVM blockchain networks. We define DLT interoperability as three capabilities:

[A]

Transfer assets

The ability to transfer assets from one DLT network to another DLT network.

[M]

Send & receive messages

The ability to send and receive messages across DLT networks.

[X]

Initiate actions

The ability to initiate actions across DLT networks.

How to contribute

Two ways to get involved

Specifications & guidelines development

The working group collaborates on specifications and guidelines defining requirements for DLT interoperability implementations. Contribute by joining the bi-weekly calls and by writing, reviewing and providing feedback on draft specifications.

Code and test suite

If you are a developer, contribute to the interfaces of the Crosschain Protocol Stack — a way for enterprises to create interoperable components for crosschain communications.

Contact

Non-EEA members: interested in the group's work? Contact eea-wg@entethalliance.org.
EEA members: contact the EEA Secretariat or your member council representative.
